# Code of conduct

cairn is built in the open by people who give their time to it. This is what we ask of everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project lives.

## What we expect

- Be kind and direct. Disagree with an idea by saying what is wrong with it and showing why.
- Assume the other person means well, and read what they wrote before you answer it.
- Keep it about the work. A person's identity, background or level of experience is never the subject.
- Accept a correction, and give one without scorn.
- Say when you used a tool to write something, and stand behind what you send either way.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, threats, or sexual language and attention, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sustained disruption: derailing a thread, repeating a settled argument, spam.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour breaks this, tell the maintainers privately, the way [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes, and say it is a conduct report. Say what happened, where, and link to it if you can. A report is read by the maintainers only and is not shared without your say.

## What happens next

The maintainers read every report and answer it. Depending on what happened they may ask for an edit or an apology, hide or remove a contribution, or bar a person from the project for a time or for good. A maintainer who is the subject of a report takes no part in deciding it.
